Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This policy explains what the X Cope website (the "site") stores about you, why it stores it, and how to have it removed. It covers the site and the small backend service that supports Roblox account linking. It is written to match what the site actually does, so you can check any claim here against the published code.

1. Who runs this site

The site is an independent fan project for the Roblox experience X Cope, maintained by its operator. It is not operated by, endorsed by, or affiliated with Roblox Corporation. The only contact channel is the official Discord server linked from the site; there is no contact form and no published email address.

2. What the site stores

Account information

Creating an account requires an email address and a password. These are handled by Google Firebase Authentication. The operator never sees or stores your password; Firebase receives it directly and stores a cryptographic hash. The email address is used for sign-in, for verification messages, and for password resets.

Profile details

If you set a display name, it is saved to your account and shown in the site header and on your profile page. If you upload a profile picture, the image is resized on your own device before it is uploaded, then stored on your account as a small embedded image. The site does not use cloud file storage for this.

Roblox account linking (optional)

Linking a Roblox account is optional and only happens if you choose to do it. Two methods exist, and they collect different things:

  • Username plus a verification code. You enter a Roblox username, the site reads that account's public profile (account id, username, display name and avatar image) to confirm the account exists, and you prove you control it by temporarily placing a code in your Roblox profile's "About me" field. Only public information is read.
  • Official Roblox sign-in (OAuth 2.0). If this method is enabled, you sign in on Roblox's own site and Roblox returns only your account id, username, display name, avatar image and profile link. The site requests just the openid and profile permissions and cannot read your Roblox password, email address, Robux, inventory or messages.

In both cases the resulting link is currently stored in your own browser, not on the server, so it does not follow you to another device. If official Roblox sign-in is used, the backend service receives the Roblox access and refresh tokens needed to complete the exchange; those tokens are held only as encrypted secrets in the backend and are never placed in the page or in a file.

Preferences and session state

The site remembers a few choices in your browser's local storage: the language you picked, the colour theme, the background you chose, and whether you asked to stay signed in. Your current position in the profile page is kept separately in session storage, which your browser discards when the tab is closed. None of this is sent anywhere.

3. What the site does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or remarketing identifiers.
  • No analytics or behavioural tracking. The site loads no analytics module and runs no tracking script. A measurement identifier appears in the site's Firebase configuration, but no analytics code is loaded, so it is unused.
  • No selling, renting or trading of personal information to anyone.
  • No collection of payment details, and nothing on the site costs money.
  • No access to your Roblox password, Robux, inventory or private messages.

4. Who else is involved

Running the site depends on a few outside services. They process data on the operator's behalf or because your browser contacts them directly:

  • Google Firebase Authentication — stores your account credentials and profile details, and sends verification and password-reset emails.
  • Cloudflare Workers — hosts the backend that talks to Roblox and verifies signed-in visitors. It holds the Roblox credentials as encrypted secrets.
  • Google Fonts — supplies the site's typefaces. Your browser requests the font files from Google's servers, which means Google receives your IP address and browser details as part of serving that request.
  • GitHub Pages — serves the site's files.
  • Discord — hosts the community server. Following the invite means leaving the site and becoming subject to Discord's own privacy policy.

Roblox itself processes the sign-in and public profile data described above under its own privacy policy. This site is not responsible for how those providers handle data.

5. How long it is kept

Account information is kept for as long as your account exists. Information held in your browser stays until you clear your browser storage or remove it through the site. Data is deleted when the account is deleted, apart from anything a provider retains in routine backups.

6. Deleting your data

You can remove the browser-side data yourself at any time by clearing this site's storage in your browser settings.

The site does not yet offer a self-service "delete my account" button, so removing an account is done on request. Ask in the official Discord server and the account and the profile details attached to it will be deleted. You can also ask to see what is stored, correct it, or receive a copy of it.

7. Children

The site is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 13, please use it only with the involvement of a parent or guardian, and do not create an account or link a Roblox account without their permission. A request to delete an account belonging to a child will be honoured on request through the Discord server.

8. Security

Passwords are handled by Firebase and are never stored by the site. Roblox credentials are kept as encrypted secrets in the backend and never written into the site's files. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the site changes how it handles data. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

For any question about this policy, or any request to see, correct or delete your data, use the official Discord server linked from the site's Contact section.
