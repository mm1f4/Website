About

Built for people who ship

XCOPE started as a single page and grew into a small toolkit for getting ideas online quickly. No build step, no framework, no lock-in - just clean HTML, CSS and JavaScript you can read top to bottom.

Everything here works offline, loads fast, and stays out of your way while you focus on the part that matters: the product.
